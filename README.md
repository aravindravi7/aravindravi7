### LG Energy Solution Vertech

**Technical Product Manager Co-op** · Boston, MA · May 2025 – Jan 2026 · **Runtime** (tenure): 9 months<br>
<sub><b>Genre</b> (the domain): Energy drama · “One model. A $220M bid.” · $220M data-center bid scoped</sub>

> **Logline** (the one-sentence version): Put a defensible price on a $220M data-center bid, and wrote the roadmap template LG now standardizes on.

**Box office** (results)
- $220M MAANG-affiliated data-center project scoped
- −40% edge data transmitted
- −60% manual audit effort
- −65% contract lookup time

**Credits** (what shipped): PPC framework · AEROS Cloud Edge · Contract compliance monitor · Contract AI agent

**Director’s cut** (the call I made): Replaced flat-rate estimates with a non-parametric cost–effort model. Later, shipped a fast-build contract AI agent instead of waiting on a full development cycle, because contract lookups were slowing decisions across 3 business units.

**Plot twist** (what that call cost): Speed over retrieval depth: even grounded on a Microsoft Graph semantic index, the agent occasionally missed relevant documents.

### Project Tech4Dev

**Product Manager** · Mumbai, India · May 2023 – Jan 2024 · Runtime: 9 months<br>
<sub>Genre: Social-impact ensemble · “Two platforms. 120+ NGOs.” · 120+ NGOs across two platforms</sub>

> Shipped the data, design and AI layers behind two open-source platforms serving 120+ NGOs and 1M+ beneficiaries.

**Box office**
- 120+ NGOs and 1M+ beneficiaries served
- ~40% less reporting time
- ~60% less manual data processing
- 100+ screens redesigned in about two months

**Credits:** Dalgo · Glific redesign · 30+ dashboards · Education chatbot

**Director’s cut:** As SPOC for the design agency across Glific and Dalgo, pushed for plain-language interfaces where every button does exactly what it says. On Glific, turned scattered settings pages into a single-URL panel.

### KoineArth

**Product Manager** · Bangalore, India · Oct 2021 – Apr 2023 · Runtime: 19 months · *Promoted from Business Analyst in 6 months*<br>
<sub>Genre: Web3 launch thriller · “No seed phrase required.” · $1.5M+ Q1 brand revenue</sub>

> Launched India’s first NFT e-commerce platform and drove $1.5M+ in Q1 brand revenue.

**Box office**
- $1.5M+ Q1 revenue through brand partnerships (MG Motors, MakeMyTrip)
- ~30% higher signup-to-transaction conversion
- 82% → 97% on-time delivery
- 5 → 100 recipients per giveaway

**Credits:** ngageN · Social-login wallet · Bulk giveaways · Enablement hub

**Director’s cut:** A social-login wallet created at sign-in with Google or phone OTP, with no seed phrase or browser extension, plus a one-tap transfer to MetaMask so holders could resell on marketplaces like OpenSea.

**Plot twist:** Mainstream-friendly meant platform-managed: crypto-native users wanted keys they controlled, and the one-tap MetaMask exit pulled NFTs and activity off the platform.

### Propelld

**Head of Disbursement Operations** · Bangalore, India · Oct 2019 – Nov 2020 · Runtime: 14 months · *Promoted from Operations Associate in 6 months*<br>
<sub>Genre: Fintech scale-up · “Map it. Then automate it.” · 10x: $190K → $1.9M a month, in 11 months</sub>

> Scaled disbursements from $190K to $1.9M a month, and built the team that ran it.

**Box office**
- $190K → $1.9M monthly disbursements in 11 months
- 4–5 hours → under 2 hours turnaround
- 70% of manual tasks automated, about 20 hours a week saved
- Team of 8, error rates down 30%

**Credits:** Disbursement automation · E-sign and KYC integrations · Training program

**Director’s cut:** Mapped the as-is disbursement process end to end before automating, then removed the bottlenecks with automation and third-party e-sign and KYC integrations instead of scaling on headcount alone.

**Plot twist:** Vendor integrations raised costs while control stayed in-house, but the speed was unmatched.
